Verification Process

All agents and agencies must complete a verification process before listing properties on RentHouse.

  • Agents: Must upload and validate their National Identification Number (NIN).
  • Agencies: Required to submit corporate documents including license of registration, real estate permit, and business verification documents.
  • Manual review by RentHouse compliance team to ensure authenticity and quality assurance.
